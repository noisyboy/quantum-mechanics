Kinematic E-Field Paradox (Constant Velocity)

Observe how the Electric Field of a relativistic charge perfectly anticipates its present position, while the optical image (the light) originates from the past.

Status: Running
β: 0.80
Distance Light (cΔt): 0 px
Distance Charge (vΔt): 0 px
The Physics: When you evaluate the Liénard-Wiechert potentials to find the Electric field of a charge moving at constant velocity, the retardation geometry strictly cancels out. The resulting $\vec{E}$ field vector at the observer points exactly away from the particle's present, instantaneous position—not the retarded position where the photon was emitted.
